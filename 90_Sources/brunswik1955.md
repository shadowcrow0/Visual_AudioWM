## 研究問題
心理學實驗只對**受試者**做隨機取樣與統計推論,卻把**情境/刺激**當成隨便挑的。
這樣得到的結論能說什麼?

## 結果與限制

### ⭐⭐ 單一刺激 = 「人為造成的完全混淆」(p. 194 原文)
> "This constitutes **artificially induced perfect confounding**, and may be labeled
> '**tied-variables' design** or, in short, **tied design**."

> "**Responder replication**, that is, repetition with new individuals or with the original
> individuals at other occasions, **is likewise irrelevant** … This must be pointed out in view
> of a rather deeply ingrained trend in psychology of **throwing the entire problem of
> generalization and of statistical significance onto the responder rather than onto the
> situation.**"

### ⭐ 多加受試者救不了單一刺激(p. 204 原文)
> "**As a matter of principle, individual sample situations, no matter how lifelike, cannot
> answer the funtional [sic] problem** as to the degree of perceptual constancy, even though
> by the use of responder replication or by systematic variation their results may become
> generalizable in certain directions and standardized for testing purposes. **Only
> representative design can answer this problem.**"

### ⭐⭐⭐ 他點名批判古典心理物理學,而且批的正是「固定第三變項」(pp. 195–196 原文)
> "***Classical psychophysics as pseudo-univariate design.*** … During the classical phase of
> psychophysics, however—**still strongly in evidence today**—the implicit design policy was
> **artificially to tie the distal and proximal variables**. For size this is achieved by
> holding the distance from the observer constant. A good example is the Galton bar. … This
> creates a **tied-variables design** … Note that the tying of the two variables is the direct
> result of **a celebrated device of systematic design, the holding constant of a third
> variable** (in our case, distance)."

**⚠️ 這一段對 [[自然音vs合成音_理論推論]] 的結論是最強的反面。**
「把 F1 與 F0 釘死、只動 VOT」**正是** Brunswik 說的
"the holding constant of a third variable" —— 他認為那製造出的是
**pseudo-univariate design**,不是乾淨的設計。

**→ 依 Brunswik,合成路線不是解方,而是這個錯誤的原型。**

### 雙重標準(pp. 212–213 原文)
> "it was a surprisingly widespread practice to apply only one (responder-populational) test
> of significance using [n], and to tacitly consider this test to cover ecological generality
> in the same breath."

### ⚠️ 但**不要**把本專案的取捨歸給 Brunswik(p. 202 原文)
> "**Representative design is not afraid of this spuriousness; in fact, it welcomes it** for
> the sake of the behavior supports it allows … The challenge of further isolation must be met
> by after-the-fact, mathematical means…"

**Brunswik 認為「取樣很多刺激」的代價是**預測變項之間的殘餘共線性**,
用偏相關去處理 —— 不是誤差變異變大、不是精度下降、不是檢力下降。
"idiosyncrasy" 與 "double standard" 這兩個詞在全文中根本沒出現。**

**→ 「多 token 會膨脹變異、降低檢力」這個框架是 [[clark1973]] 與
[[judd2012]]/[[westfall2014]] 的,不是 Brunswik 的。把它歸給 Brunswik 是誤引。**

### 一個獨立性事實

他的統計祖先是 Coleman (1964)。

## 回查線索
**「單一刺激 = 固定混淆」最早是誰說的?** → Brunswik (1955),而且他叫它
"artificially induced perfect confounding" / "tied design"。
**心理物理學被點名批判過嗎?** → **有**,本篇 pp. 195–196,"Classical psychophysics as
pseudo-univariate design",批的正是「固定第三變項」。
**Brunswik 認為多 token 的代價是什麼?** → **共線性**,不是變異膨脹。不要誤引。
