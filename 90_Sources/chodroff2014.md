## 研究問題
釋放 burst 的**頻譜形狀**能不能當作塞音 voicing 的線索?這個線索在三個發音部位上一樣強嗎?

## 方法與族群
三路證據:
1. **產出研究** —— 18 名 JHU 大學生,CVC 音節,六個塞音 × 十個母音
2. **語料庫研究** —— TIMIT
3. **知覺實驗兩個** —— 實驗室 + Amazon Mechanical Turk,用 cross-spliced 的 VOT × burst 形狀連續體

頻譜形狀以重心(centre of gravity, COG)等頻譜動差量化。

## 結果與限制
**摘要原文(p. 2762)**:
> "Voiceless labial and coronal stops were found to have greater energy at higher
> frequencies in comparison to homorganic voiced stops, a difference that could not be
> attributed to aspiration in the voiceless stops or modal phonation in the voiced,
> **while no consistent effect was found for dorsal stops.**"

一般討論(p. 2769):
> "Voiceless stops have higher spectral means than voiced stops, **a difference that is
> particularly strong for labials and also significant for coronal stops, but not
> consistently found for dorsals.**"

統計(p. 2766):
> "the spectral difference … **essentially nullified for dorsal stops
> (voice × dorsal = −178.20 [−260.04, −87.15], p < 0.001)**"

**發音生理的解釋(p. 2769)**:
> "Peak airflow for labial stops is estimated to occur within the first 10 ms, whereas
> airflow for dorsal stops increases slowly until it reaches a peak later on in the burst
> (Stevens, 1998). Thus, the difference in peak airflow between the voiceless and voiced
> stops at burst onset should be greater for labials than for dorsals. Assuming that the
> timing of the airflow peak is related to rate of release, coronal stops should fall
> between labials and dorsals, in agreement with our results."

## ⚠️ 引用查核:軟顎音的「無效果」是產出結果,不是知覺結果

[[winn2020]] p. 857 在一段主要在講**知覺**類別優度的段落末尾寫道:「they found no effect of burst spectrum for dorsal (i.e., velar) stops」。讀者很容易把它讀成一個知覺上的虛無結果。

**但 C&W 的知覺實驗根本沒測軟顎音**(p. 2764 原文):
> "a laboratory perception experiment evaluated the perceptual relevance of the burst
> spectrum by collecting identification responses and goodness ratings for cross-spliced
> VOT continua **at the anterior places (labial and coronal)**."

而且他們自己明白指出這是文獻缺口(p. 2763 原文):
> "Perception experiments of the type conducted by Keating (1979) and Nittrouer (1999)
> **have never, as far as we know, used continua for non-coronal places of articulation.**
> This is a notable gap in the literature, as findings from the production studies
> reviewed above suggest that the perceptual effect would be if anything stronger for the
> labial /p/ vs /b/ contrast, for which the difference in spectral means is numerically
> larger than in the coronals, and **possibly absent for dorsal /k/ vs /g/**."

**→ 「軟顎音沒有 burst 頻譜線索」在知覺層次上是 C&W 的預測,不是他們的發現。** 引用時必須寫成產出/聲學層次的陳述。

**同時注意方向**:C&W 預測這個線索在**唇音 /p/–/b/ 上最強**(頻譜均值差在數值上大於舌尖音)。對 AVWM 而言,這是一把雙面刃 —— 見 [[consonant-pair-choice]]。

**限制**:產出研究只有 18 名同一所大學的學生;知覺實驗未涵蓋軟顎音;COG 是頻譜形狀的粗略摘要。
