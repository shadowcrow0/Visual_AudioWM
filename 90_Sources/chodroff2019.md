## 研究問題
跨 100 多種語言,同一個 laryngeal 類別(long-lag / short-lag / lead voicing)在不同發音部位上的 VOT,是各自獨立變動,還是被某種一致性約束綁在一起?

## 方法與族群
100 種以上語言的類型學調查(二手彙整的 VOT 平均值)。

## 結果與限制
**摘要原文**:
> "Within broadly defined laryngeal categories (long-lag, short-lag, and lead voicing),
> VOT means were found to vary extensively. Importantly, the means for members of the same
> laryngeal series **did not vary independently but instead were highly correlated across
> languages.** The strong linear relations identified here cannot be reduced to previously
> reported ordinal relations, and provide evidence for **a uniformity constraint on
> phonetic realization: within a language, each laryngeal specification must be realized in
> approximately the same way across stops of different places of articulation.**"

## 對 AVWM 的意義
本篇是 [[winn2020]] 用來質疑經典部位序列的出處。Winn (2020) p. 854–855 原文:
> "**However, a more recent cross-linguistic study by Chodroff et al. (2019) challenges the
> long-held notion of this ordering of VOT based on place of articulation, showing a number
> of occasions where published studies have measured longer VOT for alveolar rather than
> velar stops.**"

**兩個相關但方向相反的訊息**:
1. **「唇音 < 舌尖音 < 軟顎音」這個序列不是鐵律** —— 有不少已發表研究測到舌尖音的 VOT 長於軟顎音。所以在選發音部位時,不該把經典序列當成精確的量化預期。
2. **但部位之間是高度相關的**(uniformity constraint)—— 這意味著換一個發音部位,並不會換到一個性質全然不同的維度,只是整條連續體的落點平移。**對 AVWM 而言,這降低了「選錯部位會毀掉整個設計」的風險。**(此為我的推論。)

**限制**:僅讀摘要;這是**產出**的類型學調查,不是**知覺**邊界的研究,不能直接推論知覺邊界的部位序列。EL(Express Letters)為短篇格式。
