## 研究問題
把十年的合成語音知覺與理解研究整合起來:困難的直接後果是什麼?那個後果如何往上傳到理解層次?

## 方法與族群
回顧 + 理論整合,無新資料。

## 結果與限制
**機制鏈(摘要原文)**:
> "We discuss the difficulties that synthetic speech causes for the listener and the
> evidence that the immediate result of those difficulties is **a delay in the point at
> which words are recognized.** We then argue that this delay in processing affects not
> only lexical access but also comprehension processes."

**核心理論句**:
> "Lower level phoneme- and word-recognition processes require more resources than usual
> for identifying words in synthetic speech. The result is **a drain of resources away
> from higher level comprehension processes.**"

**但關鍵限定在這一句**:
> "**In summary, because the listener does not normally have control over the rate of
> presentation of spoken language, the critical resource during spoken language
> comprehension is time.**"

**→ 瓶頸是「話語時間預算」:辨識變慢 → 在同一段話的時長內,留給高層理解的時間變少。**

**這對 AVWM 極其重要,因為它決定了顧慮的適用範圍(以下是我的推論)**:
若代價的本體是「在固定的話語時長裡塞不下所有處理」,那麼**單一 CV 音節不構成這個瓶頸** —— 沒有後續詞要趕著處理,沒有語意整合要被排擠,受試者也不受連續語流的節奏壓迫。這個機制在設計上就不適用於孤立音節。

**⚠️ 同時,這篇回顧也削弱了它自己的赤字主張**:
> "**When differences were found, they disappeared after minimal practice.**"
> "**Thus, with minimal practice, comprehension performance for synthetic and natural
> speech appear to be roughly equivalent when accuracy measures are used.**"
> "there are processing costs associated with the comprehension of synthetic speech,
> **costs that become apparent only when appropriate dependent measures are used to index
> processing load.**"

也就是說:**用正確率量,赤字在少量練習後基本消失;只有時間/負荷敏感的測量還看得到。** AVWM 的依變項是辨識正確率(GRT 的混淆矩陣),不是反應時間。

**但有一句反向的警告**:
> "Even synthetic words that are perfectly identifiable require more processing time for
> recognition."

若 AVWM 未來要分析 RT,這一條會回來咬。

**資源隨長度縮放**:
> "The total amount of time and resources devoted to word-related processes for a given
> sentence is assumed to vary directly with the number of words in the sentence."

**一筆 CV 層次的反例(⚠️ 二手,原始文獻未取得)**:回顧中轉述 Clark, Dermody & Palethorpe (1985):自然與合成 CV 音節在噪音中呈現,重複播放能改善自然音的辨識,**卻改善不了合成音** ——
> "The authors argue that repetitions failed to improve the identification of synthetic
> syllables because **the additional redundant cues were not present in the acoustic
> signal.**"

⚠️ **這是我找到唯一一筆 CV 音節層次的自然 vs 合成差異,而且正好在噪音中。** 它不是工作記憶效果,而是**線索冗餘度**效果 —— 但對 AVWM 的 SNR 路線特別相關。**原始文獻未取得,年份與出處未獨立核實,引用前必須查證。**

**限制**:1992 年的回顧,所評論的合成器皆為規則式;未涵蓋現代神經 TTS(見 [[govender2023]])。
