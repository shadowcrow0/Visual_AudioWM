## 研究問題

## 結果與限制

### Type I error 的量級(模擬)
只對受試者做 ANOVA 的錯誤率 **".086 in the best case to .616 in the worst case,
with an average error rate of .317, over six times the nominal alpha level."**

**⚠️ 而且**:"**increasing the number of participants led to greater positive bias in the
error rate.**"
**→ 加受試者讓問題更嚴重,不是更輕。**(與 [[brunswik1955]] p. 204 的主張一致。)

### ⭐⭐ 固定刺激 = 一致的偏差(討論段原文)
> "when experimenters attempt to replicate effects **using the same experimental stimuli** as
> in previous work but analyze these data using traditional procedures that ignore random
> stimulus variation, **it can never be clear whether a successful replication indicates a
> truly reliable treatment effect or merely a consistent bias in the set of experimental
> stimuli used.**"

**→ 這就是 [[自然音vs合成音_理論推論]] §5 要的那句話:
用固定刺激時,「真效果」與「刺激的一致偏差」在原則上分不開。**

### ⭐ 開頭的單一刺激例子
> "some of the harshest research critiques come when conclusions are reached, for instance,
> about reactions to African Americans when **an experimental participant has encountered a
> single African American as a stimulus** in an experimental interaction."

### ⭐⭐ 他們明文寫出本專案的那個取捨(討論段原文)
> "**Should one ensure considerable stimulus variability or should one attempt to have stimuli
> that resemble each other as closely as possible?** … As in sampling participants, **sampling
> less variable stimuli may lead to power benefits**, but more narrowly defined samples of
> stimuli also mean that one is unable to identify significant further moderators of effects
> of theoretical interest and that **the conclusions make reference only to a narrower range
> of stimuli.**"

**→ 這是文獻上對「同質刺激 vs 多樣刺激」最直接的一段陳述,而且兩邊都講。
它把代價定位在**類化範圍**,而不是**參數偏誤** ——
所以在「生態效度非優先」的前提下,它其實**支持**同質刺激。**
(這個判讀是我的。)

### 效果量沒有乾淨解
> "Because of multiple variance components which might all be considered error in this mixed
> design, **there is no way to easily specify a single standardized effect estimate.**"

**限制**:
- **社會心理學**;刺激是臉、人物、情境,不是語音。
- advance online 版,無頁碼。
- 我未通讀。
