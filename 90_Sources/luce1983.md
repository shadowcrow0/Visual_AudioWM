## 研究問題
**這是研究者印象中「Pisoni 證明合成語音吃掉工作記憶資源」的那篇。查證結果:這篇確實存在,結論方向也對,但常見的簡化說法比原文強。**

真正的問題是:合成語音比自然語音難記,是**知覺階段**的問題,還是**短期記憶的編碼/複誦**階段被額外佔用?

## 方法與族群
三個實驗,刺激為 Modified Rhyme Test 選出的單音節詞,**在 50 dB SPL 寬頻高斯白噪音中以 80 dB SPL 呈現**。
合成器:⚠️ 刺激段從未指名。**MITalk 是從受試者段推得的**(「None of the subjects had any previous exposure to synthetic speech generated by the MITalk system」),不是刺激段的直接陳述。

- **實驗 1**:25 詞清單,詞間距 1 / 2 / 5 秒,自由回憶
- **實驗 2**:**數字預載典範**(作者註明源自 Baddeley & Hitch 1974)—— 先視覺呈現 0 / 3 / 6 個數字,再聽 15 詞清單,先回憶數字再回憶詞
- **實驗 3**:10 詞清單,**依序**回憶

## 結果與限制
**摘要原文**:
> "Although free recall was consistently poorer overall for the synthetic lists at all
> presentation rates, the decrement for synthetic stimuli did not increase differentially
> with faster rates. ... Differences in ordered recall between the synthetic and natural
> word lists were substantially larger for the primacy portion of the serial position
> curve than the recency portion. These results indicate that difficulties observed in the
> perception and comprehension of synthetic speech are due, in part, to increased
> processing demands in short-term memory."

**結論句**:
> "synthetic speech is difficult to perceive and understand, relative to natural speech,
> in part because it affects the allocation of limited processing resources in short-term
> working memory."

## ⚠️ 三個必須誠實標明的弱點(這是本卡最重要的部分)

**1. 旗艦級的「數字預載」結果其實不顯著。**
> "**No interaction between voice type and preload was observed for word recall**,
> F (2, 238) = 1.11, p > 0.33"
> "the data reveal a **trend** for the digit recall to be poorer under the high preload
> condition for the synthetic relative to the natural word lists, **although the effect is
> not statistically significant.**"

**全部四個交互作用檢驗的精確值**(2026-08-12 由 PMC3513698 補齊,原本標為「需查 PDF」):

| 實驗 | 交互作用 | 統計值 | 顯著? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | voice × rate | F(1, 142) < 1.00 | 否 |
| 2 | voice × preload(詞回憶) | F(2, 238) = 1.11, p > 0.33 | 否 |
| 2 | voice × preload(數字回憶) | F(1, 119) = 2.47, p > 0.11 | 否(作者稱方向正確) |
| 3 | voice × list half × serial position | F(4, 284) = 2.65, **p < 0.05** | **是** |

**四個檢驗,三個 null。** 唯一顯著的那個是序列位置的三階交互作用 —— 也就是弱點 2 講的、
只在依序回憶作業出現的那個效果。

作者自己評價那個支持性測量:
> "the main finding that supports this claim—number of subjects recalling all of the
> digits—**is at best a crude measure of the capacity demands of synthetic speech.**"

**2. primacy > recency 的解離只出現在實驗 3。**
> "**Informal analyses of the serial position curves from Experiments 1 and 2 did not
> reveal differential effects of voice type on the primacy and recency portions of the
> curves.**"

也就是說,「合成語音傷害清單前段(編碼/複誦)」這個漂亮的結論,需要**依序回憶**這個高記憶負荷作業才出得來,自由回憶做不出來。

**3. 刺激是在噪音中呈現的**(白噪音 50 dB SPL)。這使本篇不是純粹的「合成 vs 自然」比較,而是「噪音中的合成 vs 噪音中的自然」。

## 對 AVWM 的意義
AVWM 本身是工作記憶作業,所以「合成語音是否吃 WM 資源」是實質顧慮,不是學術潔癖 —— 若成立,它會跟實驗操弄糾纏。

**但本篇的作業是 10–25 個詞的清單回憶,AVWM 的聽覺刺激是單一 CV 音節。** 本篇所指認的機制(編碼/複誦、序列位置)在單一音節上**沒有東西可以作用** —— 沒有序列順序要編碼,沒有清單要複誦。詳細論證見 [[natural-vs-synthetic-speech]]。

**限制**:1983 年的 MITalk 品質遠低於現代合成;合成器身分為推得(見上)。
精確統計值已於 2026-08-12 補齊。

⚠️ **關於合成器身分的補充**:Introduction 有一句
> "Pisoni and Hunnicutt (1980) performed several experiments on the intelligibility of
> speech generated by the MITalk unrestricted text-to-speech system."

但那是在描述**前人研究**,不是本篇的刺激來源。所以受試者段那句
(「None of the subjects had any previous exposure to synthetic speech generated by the
MITalk system」)仍是較強的證據,**本卡維持「推得」的標記**。
