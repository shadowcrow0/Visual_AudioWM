# Zuk et al. (2013) — 唯一同時出現 MBROLA 與 VOT 的論文

**出處** Zuk, J., Ozernov-Palchik, O., Kim, H., Lakshminarayanan, K., Gabrieli, J. D. E.,
Tallal, P., & Gaab, N. (2013). Enhanced syllable discrimination thresholds in musicians.
*PLoS ONE*, 8(12), e80546.
**DOI** 10.1371/journal.pone.0080546

## 為什麼收這張卡
Europe PMC 全文檢索中,**唯一**同時包含 "MBROLA" 與 "VOT" 的論文。但它是**反證**:

VOT 連續體(/ga/-/ka/,0-50 ms,5 ms 步進)是用 **Klatt** 做的:
> "All syllables were 200 ms in duration and were produced using a Klatt-based
> synthesizer [60]."

MBROLA 只在討論區出現一次,當作**尚未實現的未來方向**:
> "...the implementation of synthesis tools with more naturalistic stimuli, such as
> MBROLA [79] or interpolation of naturalistic speech, would be advantageous in future
> investigations..."

## 意義
領域裡 MBROLA 與 VOT 的唯一文字連結,是一篇**希望有人去試**的論文 —— 不是有人做過。
