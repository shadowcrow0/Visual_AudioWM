**Figure 1**

*Session Structure, Trial Sequence, and Adaptive Sound Calibration of the Audiovisual Working Memory Task*

[image]

*Note.* (A) Session structure: colour calibration (72 trials), sound calibration (72 trials),
practice (15 trials, with feedback after each response), and the main experiment
(4 blocks × 144 trials, no feedback), with a rest between parts. (B) Sequence of one
trial; an invalid trial is shown. After a 0.3-s fixation, four colour–syllable items
appeared one per corner for 1 s each (onsets 0.3, 1.3, 2.3, and 3.3 s; order and
positions randomised on every trial). An empty frame then appeared for 1 s at the
location of the cued item, after which the target item was shown inside it for 1 s.
On valid trials (two thirds) the target was the cued item; on invalid trials (one third)
it differed from the cued item in colour, in syllable, or in both. Participants selected
one of four options, each a colour with a syllable label ([bi] or [pi]), by pressing the
corresponding button of a Cedrus response pad; the response was untimed. (C) Timeline of
one trial. RT = response time. (D) Sound calibration (Part 2). All four items had the
anchor colour and differed only in signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), set at ±|*s*| dB; the
syllable of the chosen option updated the Psi posterior (Kontsevich & Tyler, 1999;
Glavan, 2022), which proposed *s* for the next trial. The right panel is a schematic of
the procedure, not participant data; the dashed lines mark the two SNR levels passed to
the practice and main blocks. Speaker icons with arcs denote high SNR (labelled [bi]);
icons with jagged lines denote low SNR (labelled [pi]). The two colours shown are ±3 ΔE00
from the anchor hue along a perceptually uniform arc and are illustrative; the values
used in the main experiment were set per participant by the colour calibration (Part 1).
Screen background is mid-grey.
