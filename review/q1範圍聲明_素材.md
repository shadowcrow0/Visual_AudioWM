## 2. Clark (1973) 的判準 —— 逐字引句

出處:*J. Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior*, 12(4), 335–359。
⚠️ 副標題是 "...in **psychological** research"(不是 psycholinguistic;已核 PDF 首頁與 Crossref)。

**判準本體**(p. 353):
> "When used in testing or supporting hypotheses, **the method of single cases has one
> quite severe requirement: The hypotheses of interest must be applicable to single
> cases**, and these are often rather strong hypotheses."

**為什麼集中趨勢假設不行**(p. 353):
> "There is no single case imaginable that suffices to disconfirm the homograph
> hypothesis. So the method of single cases is simply not applicable to such
> 'central-tendency' hypotheses."

**合併資料才需要強假設**(p. 354):
> "It is the lumping together of data, obliterating the single cases, that requires the
> strong assumption. For this to be done, the overall means must be shown to be
> representative of each instance."

**謬誤的定義**(p. 336):
> "...implicitly accepting the assumption that the 20 words they chose constitute the
> complete population of words they wish to generalize to."

## 3. 邏輯結構(寫聲明時的骨架,不是句子)

| 主張形式 | q=1 撐得起? | 理由 |
|---|---|---|
| 「用**這對刺激**時,invalid cue 使 intrusion 上升」 | ✅ | 對單一個案成立的假設;兩條件共用同一批刺激,條件差內部乾淨 |
| 「**/b/–/p/ 這個類別**的語音表徵與顏色有關」 | ⛔ | 類別層次的集中趨勢假設;Clark 判準明文排除 |
| 「voicing **這個特徵**如何如何」 | ⛔ | 同上,而且跨到特徵層次,外推更遠 |

**要小心的中間地帶**:「這對刺激」的效果若要暗示機制(語音表徵),
讀者會自動外推到類別 —— 聲明的功能就是擋住這個自動外推。

## 4. 可引用的支持數字(為什麼一對錄音撐不起類別主張)

- **檢力天花板**:8 個刺激、中等效果量 → 受試者無限多,檢力上限 ~.50;
  要 .80 需 **~16 個刺激**(Westfall, Kenny & Judd, 2014, *JEP:General*, p. 2026,全文已讀)。
- **固定刺激的複製困境**(Judd, Westfall & Kenny, 2012, Discussion,逐字):
  > "it can never be clear whether a successful replication indicates a truly reliable
  > treatment effect or merely a consistent bias in the set of experimental stimuli used."
- **合併/固定化在非線性模型是漸近偏誤**(Rouder et al., 2007, *Psychometrika*,摘要):
  > "analysis with aggregated data is not consistent. In fact, mnemonic ability is
  > underestimated, even in the large-sample limit."
- **語者內 token 變異的量級**(若審稿人問「為何不用多個自然 token」的反向素材):
  /pʰ/ 語者內 VOT SD 12–27 ms vs 聽者知覺雜訊 ≈10.7 ms
  ([[chodroff2017]];[[clayards2008]];詳見 [[token-variability-vs-perceptual-variance]])。

## 5. 有利的一面(聲明不必只是認錯)

- **兩個 DV 都是條件間比較,兩條件共用同一批刺激** —— 刺激層次的固有偏誤在
  valid 與 invalid 兩邊相同,條件差把它相消(⚠️ 前提:偏誤不與條件交互作用)。
- **GRT/知覺領域從未處理過這個問題**:Clark 被引 2,278 次,與 Ashby & Townsend (1986)
  的共同引用**恰好 1 篇**且非 GRT 論文(OpenAlex 引文網路,見
  [[Verification summary]])—— 你的聲明本身就是這個領域少見的誠實做法。
- **SDT 有先例**:Rouder et al. (2007)、DeCarlo (2011, *J. Math. Psychol.*)
  都正面處理過 item effects —— 可引為「此問題已被鄰近領域認可」的依據。
